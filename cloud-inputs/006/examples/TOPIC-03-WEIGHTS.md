# Topic 3 — evidence-based attention weights

Prepared 21 September 2026; rework 1 after `TOPIC-03-PLAN-CHECK.md`, 21 September 2026. Planning documents only. Companion: [Topic 3 teaching plan](TOPIC-PLAN-03-ENZYMES.md). Current scope: [syllabus p. 20](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=20).

## Method and comparability

Use **exactly the same 15 matched QP/MS pairs and coding rule as [Topic 1](TOPIC-01-WEIGHTS.md) and [Topic 2](TOPIC-02-WEIGHTS.md)**: five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3, one per year 2020–2024 per component. This is a purposive, component-balanced sample, not a random sample or a prediction. Count a paper once per outcome if it contains a marked core knowledge/skill demand or a clearly identified application. Count only separable relevant marking points; count an indivisible MCQ as one mark and disclose mixed demands. Optional credit is an opportunity, not a compulsory response. Outcome totals are **overlapping marked exposure**, not additive topic marks. Read QP and MS together; do not infer demand from keywords.

Topic-3-specific coding rules. An enzyme named as the vehicle of another topic's demand (lysosomal hydrolases, DNA polymerase, restriction enzymes, penicillin's target, CDK inhibitors as cell-cycle agents, carrier-protein saturation) does **not** count unless a marking point credits enzyme mechanism, rate or kinetics. Protein tertiary structure held by named interactions (S22/12 Q11) is Topic 2.3.3, not 3.1.2, even when the stem says "typical enzymes". A transporter graph whose scheme accepts "Vmax in context of transporters" (S23/21 Q3(b)(i)) is Topic 4 and is recorded below as an adjacent analogue only. The Paper 3 catalase-type investigation (S22/33 Q1) is coded to 3.1.3 for its measurement and to 3.2.1 for its pH/temperature explanation, once each; its table, hypothesis and variable points are AO3 skill marks credited to the outcome whose investigation they implement, as Topic 2 did for food tests.

Paper 5 and supplementary pairs are **outside the denominator**. The older [G03 gate](GATE-CRITERIA-9700-03-ENZYMES.md) used a different sample (five Paper 2, five Paper 4); its "3/5 C2 papers" figure is not imported. All page links are one-based PDF pages. MCQ keys validate an option, not a prose marking rubric.

## Weights and time budgets for every outcome

**8 outcomes; 61:30 final video; 96 macro beats (85 teaching/framing/recap beats + 11 COMMON MISTAKE beats).** Budget at **120 words per minute of final video**, the effective rate already accommodating ordinary visual pauses; prescribed reads and holds are not added again (the convention settled in Topic 2's check rounds).

Teaching allowances total **53:15**. They include demonstrations, anchored looking, framing/recap and the announce/show/read/correct portions of the expected error beats. Add **45 seconds of spoken talk-through per supported error** (the client's ruling of 21 September, replacing Topic 2's original 15 s): 11 × 45 s = **8:15**. A changed error count must change the budget. The source-backed register below owns all 11 errors. Zero supported errors means no invented COMMON MISTAKE claim.

The denominator is 15 overall and **5 per component**. Marks are overlapping exposure; range is per counted ledger block. **Breadth describes the syllabus teaching inventory**, not a statistical score. Time is an editorial judgment after that inventory, not marks multiplied by a constant. Share = final seconds / 3,690.

## Why tariff and teaching time diverge

**3.1.1 — 1:45; 4 beats; 1 error.** One mixed classification mark (lipase as macromolecule and extracellular enzyme). A statement lesson at the 2.3.4 length, plus the ECR's evidenced term error for "extracellular".

**3.1.2 — 10:45; 16 beats; 3 errors.** Twelve marks across five papers (three Paper 1s, two Paper 2s): activation-energy profile (S21/12 Q13), specificity from shape (S21/12 Q12, S24/12 Q8), the active-site alternatives in S21/22 Q5(d)(i), and a five-mark induced-fit account (S24/23 Q4(b)). The specimen MCQ on what determines specificity validates teaching but is outside the sample. Two named hypotheses, an energy diagram and the ES-complex vocabulary each need their own model, and three errors are supported.

**3.1.3 — 10:15; 15 beats; 1 error.** Twenty-two marks across five papers, mostly Paper 3 skill marks inside the catalase-type investigation (an 11-mark grouped block) plus the lactase graph reading and rate calculation; the specimen Paper 2 Q3(c) supplies the "outline how the progress is investigated" rubric. Two practical designs must be shown as demonstrations, and the progress curve must be read for rate. Learner hands-on practice remains additional ([syllabus p. 51](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=51): observing demonstrations does not count towards the 20 per cent).

**3.1.4 — 3:15; 5 beats; 0 errors.** No sampled mark in the enzyme sense; the colorimeter appears in sampled Paper 3 schemes only as an improvement to food-test endpoints. Supplementary Paper 5 and examiner-report evidence (calibration, filter colour, absorbance as a quantitative measure) sets the content. "Outline" caps it.

**3.2.1 — 15:45; 24 beats; 3 errors.** Twenty-five overlapping marks across seven papers, the largest paper incidence in the topic: three Paper 1 items, the lipase pH trace (W20/21), the substrate-concentration curve (S21/22), the catalase pH/temperature practical (S22/33) and the lactase enzyme-concentration explanation (S23/34). The allocation is now delivered as two linked lessons (L4a temperature and pH; L4b the three concentrations) at the same combined 15:45. Five factors are the syllabus list; the lesson must teach one causal grammar and apply it five times, not read five paragraphs. Three errors are supported, including the most-quoted Paper 3 diagnosis ("described… which gained no credit").

**3.2.2 — 7:00; 10 beats; 2 errors.** Seven marks across four papers; three of five Paper 1s test Km or Vmax and two examiner reports diagnose the same confusion (Km with Vmax; gradient with affinity). The graph-reading procedure (Vmax, ½Vmax, drop to the axis) is the graphical derivation required by the outcome; no algebraic derivation.

**3.2.3 — 6:45; 10 beats; 1 error.** Four marks across three papers; two Paper 1s and one Paper 2 part. The March 2023 report diagnoses the competitive/non-competitive confusion on the substrate-concentration graph; the June 2023 report records candidates drawing the wrong curve. Both curves are drawn on the 3.2.2 graph component.

**3.2.4 — 6:00; 12 beats; 0 errors.** No sampled marked demand; W20/21 uses alginate-immobilised lipase only as context, and the specimen Paper 2 Q3(b) credits product-separation and material-property points. The syllabus command is "investigate… and state the advantages"; the investigation is a demonstration plan and the advantages are stated at rubric strength. No error beat is invented.

**Practical assessment:** Papers 3 and 5 are 100% AO3 ([syllabus p. 13](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=13)). Enzyme investigations dominate the sampled Paper 3 set once (S22/33 Q1, 19 marks) and appear as data handling in another (S23/34 Q1(c)). The supplementary Paper 5 tasks below (catalase planning with Vmax evaluation; β-galactosidase with colorimeter, competitive inhibitor and Km/Vmax) show the planning and evaluation form. The materials list on [syllabus p. 58](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=58) names amylase and bacterial protease, calcium chloride and sodium alginate for immobilised enzymes, and plant sources of catalase; the ordinal-data example on [p. 61](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=61) is "the order in which test-tubes containing starch and iodine become colourless after adding amylase" (quoted; the separately sampled iodine test is taught as loss of the blue-black colour, not as a literally colourless sample).

**Mathematical requirements touched** ([syllabus p. 63](syllabus/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf#page=63)): "calculate the rate of change from the gradient of a straight line on a graph" and "calculate the rate of change from the gradient of a tangent to a curved line on a graph"; rate as 1/t or 1000/t (Learner Guide p. 29); proportional dilution tables (S23/51 Q1(d)(ii)). Km is read as the substrate concentration at ½Vmax (the S21/51 stem's own definition); no algebraic Michaelis–Menten derivation, no reciprocal plot. Three rate quantities are kept distinct throughout: initial rate (tangent gradient at t = 0, or a genuinely straight initial section), average rate (change ÷ whole interval, as in S23/34's 5.4 g min⁻¹), and 1/t as a relative rate proxy for a fixed endpoint.
