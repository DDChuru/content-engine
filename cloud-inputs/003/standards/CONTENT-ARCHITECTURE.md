# Content architecture — Biology 9700

**Decided 20 September 2026.** Supersedes any structure inherited from SaveMyExams.

## 1. Our content mirrors the syllabus, 1:1

Durai: *"the best Biology textbook actually just mirrors the syllabus, I want us to do the same."*

The production tree IS Cambridge's tree:

```
Topic (1-19)  ->  Subtopic (4.1, 4.2 ...)  ->  Outcome (4.1.4)  ->  our explanation(s)
```

Consequences, all of them good:
- **Coverage becomes a real number.** "142 of 259 outcomes" means something. "142 of 239 leaves" measured progress against a revision site's taxonomy.
- **A student recognises the shape.** It matches their syllabus, their teacher's scheme of work, and the endorsed textbook.
- **Nothing is orphaned.** Every explanation has an outcome; every outcome has a home.
- **Gaps are visible by construction** — including the practical and mathematical requirements (syllabus pp.53-67) that a topic-only structure hides.

## 2. The order of authority — and the failure we corrected

Durai: *"the danger with the approach we had taken was that it was perhaps too narrow onto the actual questions."*

He is right. Our first pass ran **mark schemes -> gate criteria -> video**, which puts the exam upstream of the teaching and produces exam coaching, not understanding.

Corrected ordering:

| Stage | Source | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1. SCOPE | **Syllabus** | what to cover, how deep, at what command level |
| 2. EXPLANATION | **Us** — Claude's knowledge, Durai's expertise, the endorsed textbook | why it is true, how to make it land |
| 3. VALIDATION | **Mark schemes, examiner material** | does a student taught this way earn credit, and are they inoculated against the known misconceptions |

**Mark schemes are a CHECK, never a source.** Teach the topic properly, then verify. Never read what is asked and build backwards.

## 3. Ceiling of SCOPE, not ceiling of UNDERSTANDING

A distinction the earlier spec blurred, and the blur is what produced a narrow video.

- **Scope ceiling — absolute.** Do not teach material outside the syllabus. No second-messenger cascades on an outline-level signalling outcome. Cambridge states many of these limits itself: *"knowledge of solute potential and pressure potential is not expected"* (4.2.6, p.22).
- **Depth of understanding — not capped.** Explaining WHY complementary shape produces specificity adds no examinable content. It is the same material, understood rather than memorised.

**A command word governs the exam's phrasing and the breadth of ground, not how well the student may understand it.** A student who understands can outline. A student who has memorised an outline can do nothing else.

## 4. Sources and their roles

| Source | Role | Held |
|---|---|---|
| **Cambridge syllabus 2025-2027** | the spine — 259 outcomes, depth qualifiers, prior knowledge, practical and mathematical requirements | `syllabus/`, mapped |
| **Past papers + mark schemes** | validation: what earns credit | 190 pairs on B |
| **Examiner reports + candidate responses** | validation: how students actually fail | 18 PDFs, 822 pages |
| **Endorsed textbook** (CUP Coursebook 5th ed) | **explanation at the right level — the layer no other source supplies** | to purchase |
| SaveMyExams | a second calibration point, consulted per outcome | mapped, demoted |

Note what changed: SaveMyExams began as our structure and is now one comparison point among several. The syllabus took its place.

## 5. What this means for the section 04 pilot

Measured against outcome **4.1.4** (p.21), the pilot is well made and mis-scoped:

| | Syllabus asks | Pilot did |
|---|---|---|
| Command | **outline** | explained in depth |
| Stages | secretion -> transport -> binding | binding only |
| Position | one role of membrane proteins among six | the entire video |

Rebuild: cover all three stages because that is the scope, explain each so it makes sense rather than listing them, and let specificity be one well-explained idea inside it rather than the whole subject.

Nothing is wasted — diagram, pipeline, structure, voice and branding all hold.
