Chemical Concentration Verification

A supervisor does not meet CCV on a chemistry screen. They meet it on the day's certificate: a coral strip that names three dosing points past their window, before an auditor does. Tap it and the Chemical hub tells you what is due, what was done, and what failed. Issue a chemical and the standard is already waiting at the station. Measure, read, record — at the time. The second pair of eyes, on the Bill of Health.

Audience: shift supervisors, SHEQ and the regional champions who lead on-site training. Daniel's voice. Landscape 1920×1080, phone-framed stills with zoom crops, Ecowize bookends. Target 4:30. Spoken numbers written as words for the voice engine. Refreshes the July video to the app as it is today.

The line the video exists to land
The label says what the drum should be. Verification says what it is. A reading taken at the station, against a range the operator did not choose, recorded at the time — that is the second pair of eyes. And when it fails, the app does not let the shift look away: corrective action, re-test, then a named site manager. The day's certificate tells you before an auditor does.
Terminology · from the July review · binding for every script line

Retired

"Load drum" is gone from the voice and the captions. On dosing stations the issue screen records litres of chemical issued, nothing else. Truth check: the current build's station picker (still 07) still shows LOAD DRUM pills on the Multiline and Super Line stations — do not ring them, and raise it with the app team before capture day.

Manual dilution

The operator enters both the chemical and the water. The app computes the percentage. Never "the water is worked out for you". Batch / lot number is required on every issue. Neat volume has an L / ml switch.

The three methods

Titration drops, conductivity, test strips. The app's own labels are the only labels: methodLabel gives "Titration (drops)", "Conductivity", and for the other two "PPM" and "Visual confirm". Say what the screen says.

Scope and cadence

Every issue carries a scope: Entire factory or Department. Verification is tracked per dosing point against the CCV cadence; the hub's due band and the Bill of Health strip both read from it.
