## Source-review flags

- No numerical error was found in the visible worked calculations. In particular, the positive-area total \(140+400+50=590\text{ m}\), the gradients \(0.6\text{ m s}^{-2}\), \(1.2\text{ m s}^{-2}\), and \(-4\text{ m s}^{-2}\), and the equal \(30\text{ m}\) and \(-30\text{ m}\) regions are consistent with the drawn values.
- “Area under the graph” is used as shorthand in the recording. It is safer to say **signed area between the graph and the time axis**: below-axis regions subtract from displacement but add by magnitude to distance.
- The PDF says average speed can only take a positive value. More precisely, speed is non-negative: average speed can be \(0\) if no distance is travelled.
- A zero-axis contact means instantaneous rest. It signals a change of direction only when the graph actually crosses the axis; merely touching the axis and returning to the same side does not.
- The slide correctly distinguishes acceleration \(-4\text{ m s}^{-2}\) from a deceleration magnitude of \(4\text{ m s}^{-2}\). In general, negative acceleration is not automatically deceleration: an object with negative velocity and negative acceleration is speeding up backwards.
