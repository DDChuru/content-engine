## Source-review flags

- At f088, the source calls the box’s weight and the table’s normal reaction a Newton’s third-law pair. This is wrong: both forces act on the box. The actual partners act on different bodies—the box pulls the Earth while the Earth pulls the box, and the box pushes the table while the table pushes the box.
- At f067, \(g\) is called “the gravitational constant” while also being described as acceleration due to gravity. \(g\) is the local acceleration due to gravity, or gravitational field strength; the universal gravitational constant is a different quantity, \(G\).
- At f032, all connectors in mechanics are described as inextensible. That is only a modelling assumption when the question specifies an inextensible string, rod or coupling; real connectors and other models may extend.
- The driving-force wording at f090 implies that a driving force necessarily produces acceleration. A vehicle can also have a non-zero driving force at constant speed when resistive forces balance it.
- The friction section correctly gives its direction but never states the Cambridge rough-contact magnitude model \(F\leq\mu R\), with equality at limiting friction (and usually while sliding in the simplified model).
