## Source-review flags

- Throughout the video and supplied PDF, velocity and acceleration units are typeset as “ms⁻¹” and “ms⁻²”. Standard 9709 notation should separate the unit symbols as \(\text{m s}^{-1}\) and \(\text{m s}^{-2}\); “ms” otherwise denotes milliseconds.
- At f103-f124, “It starts 1 metre away from the origin” determines \(|s(0)|=1\), not necessarily \(s(0)=1\). The calculation is valid only if the particle starts one metre on the positive side of the chosen origin.
- At f148-f155, the local positive peak is labelled “maximum speed” and found from \(dv/dt=0\). A complete maximum-speed test must compare \(|v|\) at all relevant stationary points and interval endpoints; a negative velocity can have the greatest speed. The later side graphs at f156-f175 supply these qualifications, but the central label is unsafe in isolation.
- At f236, the worked-example stem contains the grammatical typo “in given by”; it should read “is given by”.
- The video sometimes says “total displacement” (f176-f202). “Displacement over the interval” is clearer because displacement is already a signed net change; unlike distance, its positive and negative contributions cancel.
- No substantive content was found beyond Cambridge 9709 Mechanics section 4.2. The differentiation, integration, velocity-time graph, instantaneous-rest, extrema, displacement/distance and average-speed material all fits the stated straight-line kinematics scope using Paper 1 calculus techniques.
