## Source-review flags

- At f065, the ship’s speed changes from 8 metres per second in the scenario to 24 metres per second in the explanation. This is a clear internal typo.
- The broad statement that scalars “only take positive values” is unsafe outside the narrow examples used. Scalars have no direction, but some scalar quantities can be signed. For this mechanics lesson, distance, speed, mass, time and energy are normally treated as non-negative.
- The dog row uses \(v\) for speed. That convention is common when \(v\) means the magnitude of velocity, but the scalar/vector distinction should remain explicit.
