## S02 — Resultant force along a line
**Duration:** 49 seconds
tempo: slow
voiceSpeed: 0.9
**Maps to:** O1. Source f006–f037; sign convention from NOTES.md.
**Narration:** “The resultant is the combined effect of all the forces. Along one line, direction matters. Choose right as positive. The rightward force is fifty newtons; the leftward force is twenty newtons. [pause 0.6 s] Write resultant equals right minus left. [pause 1 s] Fifty minus twenty gives thirty newtons, to the right. [hold 2 s] Draw that resultant separately. It replaces the pair; it is not a third force. Now make the opposing forces forty newtons each. [pause 0.8 s] Their resultant is zero newtons. [hold 2 s] This is equilibrium: forces act, but their effects balance.”
**Viewer sees:** Box with source 50 N right, 20 N left, positive arrow. Handwrite R = F right − F left before 50 − 20 = 30 N. Replace working panel with a separate resultant diagram (never add a third force). Change original pair to 40 N each at the spoken cue, then replace the resultant arrow with 0 N. Two short holds after the numerical results.

## S05 — Balance the forces
**Duration:** 67 seconds
tempo: slow
voiceSpeed: 0.9
**Maps to:** O2. Source f066–f097; what-if beat required by §4.
**Narration:** “Start with the principle: resultant equals zero. [pause 2 s] In symbols, U minus D equals zero, so U equals D: total upward force equals total downward force. [pause 2 s] Substitute from the diagram. Ten plus, bracket, eighteen plus x, equals five x. [hold 2 s] Collect the constants: twenty eight plus x equals five x. [hold 2 s] Subtract x from both sides: twenty eight equals four x. [hold 2 s] Divide by four. x equals seven. [hold 2 s] The upward forces are now ten and twenty five newtons; together, thirty five. Downward is also thirty five newtons. [hold 2 s] The resultant is zero newtons. Equilibrium told us that immediately. [hold 2 s] What if the downward pull increased? The forces would no longer balance.”
**Viewer sees:** Same complete given diagram; lined paper starts with “Resultant = 0”, then “U − D = 0; U = D”. U/D definitions accompany the corresponding arrow groups. Successive handwritten lines 10 + (18 + x) = 5x, 28 + x = 5x, 28 = 4x, x = 7, R = 0 N. Ring each source during substitution. When checking, keep original expressions and append their evaluated values to the corresponding diagram label; label totals 35 N only as narrated. Increase downward arrow length during the final what-if; label it “increased pull”, not the old 35 N. Formula and result remain through the answer, then a compact caption replaces paper for the hypothetical.

## S07 — Equilibrium can move
