## Source-review flags

- At f061-f067, “If both particles move left after collision 1, there will not be another collision” is false in general and contradicts the valid relative-speed condition shown at f050-f054. With \(A\) left of \(B\) and both moving left, \(B\) catches \(A\) if \(B\)'s leftward speed is greater. If the symbols are signed velocities with right positive, the condition is \(w_B<v_A\); \(w_B>v_A\) is correct only when both symbols mean positive speed magnitudes in the leftward direction.
- At f068-f140, the worked example calls the spheres “identical” while assigning them different masses. Identical uniform spheres would have equal mass; “uniform spheres of equal radius but different masses” or simply “three spheres” would be consistent.
- Throughout the worked example, the speed unit is typeset as “ms⁻¹”. Standard 9709 notation should separate the units as \(\text{m s}^{-1}\), since “ms” otherwise denotes milliseconds.
- The wall example is usable only with additional information about the rebound. Momentum of particle \(B\) alone is not conserved during its collision with a fixed wall because the wall exerts an external force. A restitution law would be beyond the stated 9709 scope.
