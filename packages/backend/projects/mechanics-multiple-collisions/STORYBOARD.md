## Presentation and timing contract

- Same graphite, ivory paper, neutral ink, single green accent and graphite fade-through as MechanicsModellingAssumptions and MechanicsDrawingTravelGraphs. Plain headers; no captions, interface chrome or decorative labels.
- At most three regions including the header: one track/diagram and one card or lined-paper panel. At most twelve words per card or handwritten line. One new number/annotation per spoken cue. Replace the diagram between collisions; never pile three diagrams on screen.
- S03 is an animated story before any equations: actual sphere motion and contact, velocity arrows proportional to speed, a brief contact flash; no numerical labels. These are spheres, never “identical spheres”.
- Each worked collision opens with its plain signpost, a 1.5-second silent card hold, then a one-breath situation/goal/idea setup ending “let's get drawing”. The bare diagram can be present, but no givens or working appear before that phrase finishes.
- Two slow scenes, S04 and S06, ElevenLabs speed 0.9; other scenes 1.0. Written bracketed directions are inserted silence, never spoken. Write at pen pace, then ring completed results and freeze for two seconds. S05 has a three-second understanding hold.
- Use right as positive throughout. Unknowns are v_B after A–B and w_B after B–C, with true subscripts on the diagram and paper. Units are m s⁻¹; momentum units kg m s⁻¹ if shown.
- §7 is applied by ending on the complete worked collision sequence and the next-collision decision. Do not add a displacement graph absent from the recording.

## S04 — Collision 1: A with B

**Narration:** “Now collision one. [hold 1.5 s] Find B's velocity after A hits it. Right is positive. Let's get drawing. A: one kilogram, four metres per second. B: two kilograms, three metres per second. Afterwards A moves at two metres per second. Call B's new velocity v B. The pair's momentum is conserved because no external force changes its total. Before equals after: one times four, plus two times three, equals one times two, plus two v B. [hold 2 s] Ten equals two plus two v B. [hold 2 s] Subtract two and divide by two: v B is four metres per second. [hold 2 s] Positive means right. Carry that four forward, not the original three.”

## S06 — Collision 2, then the next collision

**Duration:** 80 seconds
tempo: slow
voiceSpeed: 0.9
**Maps to:** O1–O3. **Source:** f106–f140; complete worked close required by §7.

**Narration:** “Now collision two. [hold 1.5 s] B hits C. Find B's new velocity; use the result just found. Let's get drawing. B: two kilograms, now four metres per second. C: three kilograms, one metre per second. Afterwards C moves at three metres per second. Call B's new velocity w B; v B belonged to the first collision. Conserve this pair's momentum. Two times four, plus three times one, equals two w B, plus three times three. [hold 2 s] Eleven equals two w B plus nine. [hold 2 s] Subtract nine and divide by two: w B is one metre per second. [hold 2 s] Now: will they collide again? [hold 1.5 s] A is behind B. A moves right at two metres per second; B at one. Two is greater than one. [hold 2 s] The one behind is faster, so the gap closes. Yes, A catches B again.”
