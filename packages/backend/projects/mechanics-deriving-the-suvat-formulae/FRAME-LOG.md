## Source-review flags

- At f027-f065, the velocity and acceleration units are written as “ms⁻¹” and “ms⁻²”. Standard Cambridge notation should separate the unit symbols as \(\text{m s}^{-1}\) and \(\text{m s}^{-2}\); “ms” conventionally denotes milliseconds.
- At f154-f164, the route to \(v^2=u^2+2as\) rearranges \(v=u+at\) as \(t=(v-u)/a\), which divides by \(a\) without mentioning the \(a=0\) case. The final identity remains true for zero acceleration, but this particular derivation requires \(a\ne0\); when \(a=0\), \(v=u\) and the identity follows directly.
- At f189-f246, the right-hand integration illustration starts numerically with \(a=5\), \(v=\int5\,dt\), \(v=5t+c\), and later \(s=\int(10+5t)\,dt\), but its red initial-condition working switches back to symbolic \(a\) and \(u\). It therefore does not remain a coherent numerical example.
- At f232-f246, the displayed zero-time substitution is written \(0=u\times0+a\times0+d\). From the preceding \(s=ut+\tfrac12at^2+d\), the line should retain the term as \(\tfrac12a(0)^2\). The conclusion \(d=0\) is unchanged, but the written substitution drops the factor \(\tfrac12\) and square.
- No material beyond Cambridge 9709 section 4.2 was identified. The f177-f249 integration route is ancillary to the requested velocity–time graph derivation, but time differentiation/integration is within the section's syllabus scope.
