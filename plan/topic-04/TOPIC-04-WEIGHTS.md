## Method and comparability

Topics 1–3 used one fixed, component-balanced sample of 15 matched QP/MS pairs (five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3) and a question-level ledger read from the PDFs. This run had no PDFs; its first draft rested on the Topic 4 gate file (G04) and `EXAMINER-INSIGHT-9700.md`. **The plan check then reopened the seven cited QP/MS pairs, the June 2023 examiner report and the official June 2024 report, and supplied exact contexts, marks and quotations for the cited parts**; this rework adopts them. The weights rest on two things, in this order:

1. **Syllabus breadth and teaching load** (primary): command words, the number of named processes, components and investigations, and the explicit depth qualifiers (4.2.2's required inclusion; 4.2.6's exclusion).
2. **Exposure within the cited question blocks** (secondary, validation only) of G04's five component 2 papers (W20/21, S21/22, W22/23, S23/21, M24/22), with the two component 5 papers (W20/51, M24/52) as supplementary practical evidence outside the counts.

**Coding rules.** Every row is coded to syllabus outcomes by what the part assesses, as the plan check describes it. A part may be coded to more than one outcome (overlapping exposure). **Direct** exposure is kept separate from **adjacent** contexts (a cytoplasmic receptor for specificity; a mixed phloem mass-flow block for osmosis). Parts outside Topic 4 (M24/22 Q1(a)(i) glycerol; Q1(c)(i) SER context; Q1(c)(ii) enzyme action) are recorded and excluded. Quotations verified by the plan check are tagged **PDF-CHECKED (plan check)**. Exposure figures are **overlapping opportunities within the cited question blocks, not additive topic marks, a full re-audit of every question in those papers, or archive-wide frequencies** (plan check). No full matched-sample manifest is claimed; the distinction from Topic 3's component-balanced sample stands.
