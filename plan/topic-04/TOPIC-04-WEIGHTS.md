## Method and comparability

Topics 1–3 used one fixed, component-balanced sample of 15 matched QP/MS pairs (five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3) and a question-level ledger read from the PDFs. **This topic does not reproduce that method.** The weights rest on two things, in this order:

1. **Syllabus breadth and teaching load** (primary): command words, the number of named processes, components and investigations, and the explicit depth qualifiers (4.2.2's required inclusion; 4.2.6's exclusion).
2. **G04's cited question blocks** (secondary, validation only): G04's sample is **15 matched QP/MS pairs: five component 2, five component 4, two component 3, two component 5 and one component 1, 2020–2024**; its counting denominator is **the five sampled component 2 papers** (W20/21, S21/22, W22/23, S23/21, M24/22). The cited Topic 4 parts of those papers, and of the two component 5 papers, were **checked against the actual QP/MS PDFs by the independent plan check (run 006)**, which re-coded several parts; the ledger below is that verified coding. A paper counts once per outcome if a cited part carries an awarded-mark opportunity for that outcome. Component 5 papers (W20/51, M24/52) are **outside the denominator** and recorded as supplementary practical-design evidence ("This provides unusually strong practical-design evidence, not a higher C2 frequency", G04). G04 cites no Topic 4 part in its component 1, 3 or 4 papers; that is recorded as "none cited", not as proof of absence.

**Coding rules.** Every row is coded to syllabus outcomes by what the part actually assesses (the plan check's reading of the QP/MS). A part is coded to every outcome whose content it assesses (overlapping exposure), as Topic 3 did. **Direct** exposure means the part assesses that outcome's teaching scope; **adjacent** means the part touches it from another context (a cytoplasmic receptor; phloem mass flow) and is recorded separately, never added to the direct totals. No Paper 5 marks enter the Paper 2 totals. Totals are overlapping opportunities within the cited blocks, not additive topic marks, not a full re-audit of every question in those papers and not archive-wide frequencies.

**Citation status.** Rows marked **PDF-CHECKED (plan check)** were checked by the independent plan check against the PDFs (QP/MS/ER pages as given; one-based PDF pages). The June 2024 examiner report (R24) was checked from the official Cambridge download. No direct exam quotation in these documents remains unchecked.
