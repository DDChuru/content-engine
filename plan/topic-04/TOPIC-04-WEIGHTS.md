## Method and comparability

Topics 1–3 used one fixed, component-balanced sample of 15 matched QP/MS pairs (five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3) and a question-level ledger read from the PDFs. **This run cannot reproduce that method:** the exam PDFs are offline, and the only question-level evidence available is the Topic 4 gate file (G04, `GATE-CRITERIA-9700-04-CELL-MEMBRANES-AND-TRANSPORT.md`) and `EXAMINER-INSIGHT-9700.md`. The weights below therefore rest on two things, in this order:

1. **Syllabus breadth and teaching load** (primary): command words, the number of named processes, components and investigations, and the explicit depth qualifiers (4.2.2's required inclusion; 4.2.6's exclusion).
2. **G04's sampled incidence** (secondary, validation only): G04's fixed sample is **15 matched QP/MS pairs: five component 2, five component 4, two component 3, two component 5 and one component 1, 2020–2024**, and its counting denominator is **the five sampled component 2 papers** (w20_21, s21_22, w22_23, s23_21, m24_22). A paper counts once per outcome if a cited part carries an awarded-mark opportunity for that outcome. Marks are recorded **only where G04 states them**; otherwise "not stated". Component 5 papers (w20_51, m24_52) are **outside the denominator** and recorded as supplementary practical-design evidence, as G04 does ("This provides unusually strong practical-design evidence, not a higher C2 frequency"). G04 cites no Topic 4 part in its component 1, 3 or 4 papers; that is recorded as "none cited", not as proof of absence.

**Coding rules.** G04 section names follow the SaveMyExams map, not syllabus paragraphs; every row below is re-coded to syllabus outcomes by what the cited part assesses (G04's description). A part is coded to every outcome whose content it assesses (overlapping exposure), as Topic 3 did. A receptor-specificity part is coded to 4.1.4 (stages and binding) and 4.1.3 (receptor role). A "why does this substance need a transport protein" part is coded to 4.1.3 (permeability, transport) and 4.2.1 (facilitated diffusion). Osmosis in phloem (w20_21) is coded to 4.2.6 as **adjacent** (the phloem context is Topic 7). G04 lists s21_22 Q3(b) and Q5(b)(ii) under "membrane transport or receptor specificity" without describing them; they are recorded as **uncoded** (content not described), and s21_22 still counts for 4.1.3/4.1.4 through Q3(c). Every exam citation here is **PDF-UNCHECKED**. Totals are overlapping exposure, not additive topic marks.

**Five-move requirement:** announce; show written error with a persistent COMMON MISTAKE or EXAM CONTRAST marker (same colour, same timing); anchored silent read 3–4 s; spoken talk-through identifying where the error is, why students make it (our teaching hypothesis, said as a possibility) and why it loses the mark (quoting the evidence where it exists); correct in place and finish on the correct state; when the written answer has several faults the marker clears only on the completed correct frame. The wrong proposition is never spoken as fact; a wrong word is named only as the word under discussion. Each error beat as written lands at 65–75 s and is never thinned.
