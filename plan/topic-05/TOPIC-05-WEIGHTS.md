## Method and comparability

The method is **exactly the Topic 1–3 method**: the same 15 matched QP/MS pairs, five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3, one per year 2020–2024 per component (s20_12, s21_12, s22_12, s23_12, s24_12; w20_21, s21_22, w22_23, s23_21, s24_23; s20_33, s21_33, s22_33, s23_34, w24_34). Count a paper once per outcome if it contains a marked core knowledge/skill demand or a clearly identified application. Count only separable relevant marking points; count an indivisible MCQ as one mark exposure (a distractor alone does not create extra marks); an MCQ relevant to two outcomes is one exposure to each, disclosed as shared. Outcome totals are **overlapping marked exposure**, not additive topic marks. Read QP and MS together; do not infer demand from keywords.

**History of this ledger.** The initial weights were a partial G05 transcription: four of the five fixed-sample Paper 2 pairs, with several tariffs unknown, and "verified tariffs" meant *transcribed from G05*, not verified against PDFs. The independent PDF check resolves the cited tariffs and identifies additional fixed-sample questions listed in CHECK.md. Update the ledger and recompute distinct-paper incidence, separable marks and mixed-demand ownership before presenting full-sample attention weights. Outcome totals overlap and are not additive topic marks. The 58:30 allocation remains an editorial teaching budget, provisionally retained; frequency does not mechanically set runtime. **This revision does that recomputation** from the check's PDF-verified rows (below).

## Weights and time budgets for every outcome

Teaching allowances total **50:20**; error beats **7 × 1:10 = 8:10**. A changed error count must change the budget.
