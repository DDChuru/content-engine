## Method and comparability

The intended method is **exactly the Topic 1–3 method**: the same 15 matched QP/MS pairs, five each from Papers 1, 2 and 3, one per year 2020–2024 per component (s20_12, s21_12, s22_12, s23_12, s24_12; w20_21, s21_22, w22_23, s23_21, s24_23; s20_33, s21_33, s22_33, s23_34, w24_34). Count a paper once per outcome if it contains a marked core knowledge/skill demand or a clearly identified application. Count only separable relevant marking points; count an indivisible MCQ as one mark and disclose mixed demands. Outcome totals are **overlapping marked exposure**, not additive topic marks. Read QP and MS together; do not infer demand from keywords.

**What this run could and could not do.** With the PDFs offline, the only question-level evidence is G05's, whose sample differs: "five component 2, five component 4, two component 3, two component 5 and one component 1, spanning 2020–2024" (G05, Basis and limits). **Four of G05's five C2 pairs are fixed-sample Paper 2 pairs** — w20_21, s21_22, w22_23, s23_21 — so the Paper 2 column below is populated from G05 for those four. The fifth fixed-sample Paper 2 pair, **s24_23**, and **all five Paper 1 and all five Paper 3 fixed-sample pairs are `UNVERIFIED — conductor to code against Topic 5`**. G05's own C2 pair m24_22 (March 2024) is not a fixed-sample pair and is recorded in the supplementary ledger. G05's headline "Cell cycle, chromosome behaviour or uncontrolled division: 5/5" is G05's own C2 denominator (it includes m24_22), and is quoted as that, not as a fixed-sample figure.
